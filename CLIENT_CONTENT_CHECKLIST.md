# Client Content & Operational Asset Checklist
**Client Project:** Chimbetete Rubber & Hose (Zimbabwe B2B Digital Platform)  
**Document Purpose:** Onboarding audit of verified corporate credentials, inventory manifests, branch logistics, and digital assets required for final production deployment.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Legal Credentials
- [ ] **Official Company Name & Trading Name:** Confirmed registered legal entity name in Zimbabwe.
- [ ] **Company Registration & Tax Details:**
  - BP / TIN / VAT Registration Number for statutory tax invoicing notices.
  - Standard payment terms (e.g., Strictly COD, 30-day corporate account terms).
- [ ] **Company History & Profile:**
  - Verified year of establishment.
  - Accurate summary of company background, milestones, and engineering heritage (to replace demo profile text in `/about`).
- [ ] **Corporate Logo Files:**
  - High-resolution vector logo (`.SVG`, `.AI`, or `.EPS` preferred; high-res transparent `.PNG` minimum 2000px).
  - Favicon or icon mark.
  - Brand style guide (official corporate Pantone / CMYK / HEX color codes if divergent from current safety orange `#FF5500`).

---

## 2. Depot Locations & Regional Trade Desks
For each operating branch (e.g. Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Kwekwe):
- [ ] **Exact Physical Street Address:** Stand / building / industrial area name (e.g. Graniteside, Nyakamete, Belmont).
- [ ] **Official Landline Telephone Numbers:** Main switchboard numbers.
- [ ] **Direct WhatsApp Trade Lines:** Dedicated business mobile numbers assigned to sales counters.
- [ ] **Branch Email Inboxes:** Dedicated branch sales email (e.g. `harare@company.co.zw`).
- [ ] **Counter Operating Hours:** Standard weekday and Saturday morning counter opening times.
- [ ] **After-Hours / Breakdown Hotline:** 24/7 mobile number for emergency breakdown assistance.

---

## 3. Product Catalogue & Inventory Manifest
- [ ] **Active Inventory Spreadsheets:** Excel / CSV exports of products stocked or custom-supplied:
  - Product Name & Category
  - Manufacturer Part Numbers / Internal SKUs
  - Available Nominal Diameters / Section Profiles (e.g., 1/4" to 2" for hoses; SPZ, SPA, SPB, SPC, A, B, C for belts)
  - Working Pressure & Burst Pressure ratings (Bar / PSI)
  - Temperature envelopes (Min / Max °C)
  - Tube & Cover materials (NBR, EPDM, Natural Rubber, PVC)
  - Standard coil or roll lengths (e.g., 30m, 50m, 100m)
- [ ] **Custom Assembly Capabilities:**
  - Types of crimping presses available in-house (e.g., up to 2" 4-spiral hydraulic).
  - Hose fitting thread standards stocked (BSP, JIC, ORFS, Metric, DIN, NPT, Camlock).
  - Conveyor belt mechanical splicing tools stocked (Alligator, bolt solid plate).

---

## 4. Brand Distributorships & Quality Certifications
- [ ] **Authorized Distributorship Proof:**
  - Written confirmation of official distributorship or commercial reseller status for specific brands (e.g. Gates, Fenner, Dunlop, Manuli, Parker, Linatex, Flexco).
  - *Rule:* Only officially documented relationships will be displayed as authorized partners.
- [ ] **Quality & Safety Certifications:**
  - ISO certificates (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001) if formally awarded.
  - Standards Association of Zimbabwe (SAZ) marks or approvals.
  - SANS / Mine Health & Safety compliance documentation.

---

## 5. Technical PDF Datasheets & Manuals
- [ ] PDF specification sheets for hydraulic hoses, mining slurry lines, and belt cross-reference tables.
- [ ] Chemical resistance guides or hose maintenance protocols to link directly to product detail pages.

---

## 6. Photography & Workshop Media
- [ ] **Real High-Resolution Facility Photos:**
  - Counter trade desks and warehouse racking.
  - Workshop technicians crimping hydraulic hose assemblies.
  - Bulk conveyor belting rolls and hose coils ready for dispatch.
  - Delivery fleet vehicles.
  - *Requirement:* Authentic, high-resolution photographs representing practical engineering expertise (avoiding generic corporate stock photography).

---

## 7. Commercial Billing & Banking Details
- [ ] **Bank Settlement Accounts:**
  - Nostro USD Bank Account details (Bank Name, Branch, Account Number, Swift Code).
  - ZiG RTGS / Interbank settlement account details.
- [ ] **Official Sales Notification Inboxes:**
  - Email addresses that must receive immediate notifications when a website visitor submits an RFQ (e.g., `quotes@company.co.zw`).

---

## 8. Domain, DNS & Hosting Infrastructure
- [ ] **Domain Registrar Access:** Login or DNS management access for the company domain (e.g. `company.co.zw` via ZISPA / Webdev / Namecheap).
- [ ] **Email Delivery Service Credentials:**
  - SMTP access or API keys for transactional notifications (e.g. SendGrid, Resend, Mailgun, or corporate Exchange / Google Workspace).
- [ ] **Google Search Console & Google Business Profiles:**
  - Access to claim Google Business Profile locations in Harare, Bulawayo, and Mutare for local Zimbabwe search presence.
